Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 12, 2025

🔒 Our Privacy Commitment

Remind Me is designed with privacy first. We believe your data belongs to you, and we've built this extension to respect that principle completely. All your data stays on your device.

What Data We Collect

The extension stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Selected text, links, or images you choose to save as reminders
  • URLs of pages where you set reminders
  • Optional notes/descriptions you add to reminders
  • Scheduled reminder times
  • Reminder history (last 100 reminders)

Where Your Data is Stored

100% Local Storage: All data is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local) and never leaves your device.

What We DON'T Do

  • ❌ We do NOT send your data to any external servers
  • ❌ We do NOT collect analytics or tracking data
  • ❌ We do NOT share your data with third parties
  • ❌ We do NOT require account creation or login
  • ❌ We do NOT use cookies for tracking
  • ❌ We do NOT access websites you haven't explicitly interacted with

Permissions Explained

The extension requests the following permissions:

  • storage - To save your reminders locally on your device
  • activeTab - To capture content only from tabs where you actively use the extension (right-click menu)
  • contextMenus - To add "Remind me about this" to your right-click menu
  • alarms - To schedule and trigger reminders at the times you specify
  • notifications - To show you browser notifications when reminders are due

Data Retention

  • Reminders are stored locally until you manually delete them
  • The extension keeps your last 100 reminders in history
  • You can clear all reminders at any time from the extension popup
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data

Your Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • View all your reminders in the extension popup
  • Delete individual reminders or clear all at once
  • No data recovery after deletion (it's truly gone)
  • Export functionality (planned for future release)

Changes to This Policy

We'll update this policy if we make any changes to how the extension handles data. The "Last Updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.

Open Source

This extension's code is open source. You can review exactly what it does at our GitHub repository.
