# Remind Me - Smart Web Reminders

Never forget important web content again! Remind Me is your intelligent companion for setting contextual reminders while browsing.

## 🌟 Key Features

• **One-Click Reminders**
Simply right-click any text or element on a webpage to set a reminder. It's that easy!

• **Smart Context Retention**
We save not just the reminder, but the context - including the webpage URL and selected content.

• **Flexible Scheduling**
Choose when you want to be reminded:
- Later today
- Tomorrow
- Next week
- Custom date and time

• **Beautiful Interface**
Modern, clean design with intuitive controls and smooth animations.

• **Email Notifications**
Optional email reminders ensure you never miss important information.

• **Privacy First**
Your data stays in your browser. We respect your privacy.

## 💡 Perfect For

• Students tracking research materials
• Professionals following up on articles
• Shopping enthusiasts saving products for later
• Anyone who wants to remember web content

## 🔒 Permissions Explained

• Storage: To save your reminders locally
• Active Tab: To capture the content you want to remember
• Notifications: To alert you when it's time to remember
• Email (optional): For reminder notifications
